AWS machines, measured four times a day.

LedgerMath boots 5 machine types in AWS's us-east-2 Region at 04:10, 10:10, 16:10 and 22:10 UTC, runs 14 benchmarks on each freshly booted machine, and keeps every Run with its evidence.

The figures, the evidence and AWS's On-Demand prices are a static JSON API, made for agents that choose infrastructure from measured numbers.

02

Where every figure comes from

Nothing is typed in. Every Run is kept: what the benchmark printed and what was recorded about the machine it booted on. A published, versioned Method turns a Series' Runs into its figures, and every figure links to the Runs behind it.

  1. RunOne benchmark on one freshly booted machine. Kept whole, counted or failed: its binary, its output and the machine's dumps, each stored by its SHA-256.
  2. SeriesThe Runs that share a benchmark, its settings, its build, a machine type and an Availability Zone ID.
  3. MethodVersioned and published: series v1 and requests v1. It turns a Series' counted Runs into percentiles; its definitions travel with the data, in series.json.
  4. FigureExactly as the Method's Derivation holds it. Nothing rounded, nothing computed that the Derivation does not hold.

03

For agents, and the developers who run them

Static JSON, version 1, at api.ledgermath.com. An agent reads every Series' figures with the address of its source, follows any figure down to its Runs and their evidence, and prices a machine from the same files. Version 1 only ever gains fields.

  • The table of contents: every file, its schema, the newest Run
    $ curl https://api.ledgermath.com/v1/index.json
  • Every Series (187) with its percentiles, and the Methods' definitions
    $ curl https://api.ledgermath.com/v1/series.json
  • AWS's On-Demand prices, copied 2026-11-19
    $ curl https://api.ledgermath.com/v1/prices/2026-11-19.json
